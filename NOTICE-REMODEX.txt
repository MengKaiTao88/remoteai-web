This personal Web edition adapts the presentation ideas and conservative parsing
approaches from ThinkingDisclosureParser.swift and StreamingMarkdownBlockSplitter.swift
into new TypeScript components. Changes include fenced-example handling, Remote AI item
mapping, memoized React blocks, browser dialogs and existing-controller integration.
Other composer and sidebar layouts are source-informed React/CSS adaptations.
No Remodex branding or native runtime is bundled. This notice does not imply endorsement.
